Home Marketing

The quality and comprehensiveness in home marketing has a significant and influential impact on the success of your sale — this is typically the first impression of your home and the last thing a potential buyer will look at before deciding how strong their offer will be.

Listing my Home For Sale  ·  Distinctive Home Marketing

Complimentary
01

Professional Staging

Maximize the potential of your home by taking advantage of our complimentary home staging services, which are led by professional stagers. We're looking for that strong emotional response from a buyer — when they have to have it. Professional stagers will make the home edits that result in the strongest possible offer.

Premium
02

Professional Photography

In order to capture the most attractive and effective listing photographs, I work with the best real estate photographer I could find. Top quality, professional images are the foundation to showcasing your home in the best light with the best perspectives, helping prospective buyers imagine your home's best lifestyle.

Drone & Satellite
03

Aerial Video & Imaging

To give buyers a comprehensive view of your home, property and surroundings, we use Google aerial view and drone imaging to provide a top-down, satellite perspective. These tools allow buyers to see a detailed bird's-eye view and get a better sense of its layout and location. Drone video is a must-have in top-tier marketing.

3D Virtual
04

Matterport 3D Tour

Attract more qualified leads by giving potential buyers the most complete representation of your home. Providing an immersive walkthrough with a Matterport 3D virtual tour leads to in-person viewings by buyers who already have a great sense of your layout, design, and are genuine candidates to purchase.

Transparent
05

Pre-Listing Inspection

Providing potential buyers with a pre-listing home inspection shows them that you have nothing to hide. Inspecting your home prior to listing enables you to see your home through an inspector's eyes before the buyer does, allowing you to make any repairs early and result in the cleanest possible offer.

2D & 3D
06

Bespoke Floorplans

As impressive as a 3D virtual tour is, some people prefer to see a traditional 2-dimensional blueprint style floor plan. Boutique marketing isn't defined by cutting corners, so our home marketing provides both 3D and 2D floor plan imagery to satisfy all buyers for all residential listings.

CLAR + TRREB
07

Broad Board Reach

I am a member of CLAR, which is an associate member of the Toronto Board (using the same system as TRREB). Your home will be shared with the Toronto Board and available to all local Realtors and those far outside your area, including Durham, Northumberland, Hastings, Prince Edward, and Peterborough.

Targeted

You never know where your future buyer might first see your home for sale. We use strategic social media platforms making use of targeted, paid advertising options in our home marketing system to ensure maximum reach and qualified exposure for every property.

To ensure your home receives maximum exposure, we utilize a wide network of international real estate search sites, targeting key markets in the United States, Asia, Europe, South America, and beyond. We showcase your property to a global audience across over 60 search platforms.
