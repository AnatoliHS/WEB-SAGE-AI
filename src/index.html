(01) The idea

Software you can make, not just use.

Most apps are made for you. Tinker lets you make your own: describe what you want, and it builds a small app you can play with, change and keep.

  1. 01

    Start with an idea

    Describe a game, a tool or a presentation in your own words. Tinker builds it for you, from a bill splitter to a scientific calculator.

  2. 02

    Play right away

    Tinker comes with starter apps, like a cat-and-fish game and a dinner bill splitter, that work even without an internet connection.

  3. 03

    Keep what you make

    Everything you create lives in My apps, sorted into categories, with a cover image you can update in one click.

(02) Meet Tinker

Make it. Remix it. Share it.

From a game of Go to a working copy of Winamp, the apps people make in Tinker run right inside it.

01

Discover

Browse the public gallery of apps other people have made, sorted into categories, and open any of them in a click.

02

Remix

Open someone's app to get your own copy, then change it however you like, even translate it into your language.

03

Work offline

Run Tinker offline with your own AI model. The starter apps keep working without a connection.
