Questions, answered.

Tinker Click is a desktop and iPad app for making your own apps by describing them: games, tools, documents, charts, dashboards, presentations and more.

It's like a code-sharing platform for people who don't write code. Keep what you make, remix apps other people have made, and share your own in the public gallery.

Not at all. Describe what you want in plain words and Tinker Click does the rest. If you do code, you can go further: edit the system prompt, add your own tools, or host your own server.

Yes. Everything you make stays private until you choose to share it in the public gallery.

Yes. Tinker Click is developed in the open on GitHub. You can use the official Tinker Click server or run your own.

No. You can open Tinker Click and play the built-in starter apps straight away. Create an account when you want to make and save your own, or choose to run Tinker Click offline instead.

Yes. The starter apps work offline, and you can run Tinker Click fully offline with your own AI model. Browsing the public gallery needs a connection.
