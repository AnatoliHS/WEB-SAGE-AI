(01) The idea

Software you can make, not just use.

Most software is built for everyone, so it fits no one exactly. Tinker builds a focused app for the job in front of you. Use it, keep it, or move on to the next idea. The only limit is your imagination.

  1. 01

    Start with a simple ask

    Describe the job in your own words and your own language. Tinker builds it for you, from a dinner bill splitter to a scientific calculator.

  2. 02

    Bring your own material

    Add documents, images or raw data, then shape them into a web page, an app concept, a chart, a PDF or a PowerPoint deck.

  3. 03

    Refine until it fits

    Keep asking for changes until it works exactly the way you want. Everything you make lives in My apps, ready whenever you need it again.

(02) Meet Tinker

Make it. Remix it. Share it.

Think of it as a music library for apps, where you can add your own tracks. From a game of Go to a working copy of Winamp, the apps people make in Tinker run right inside it.
