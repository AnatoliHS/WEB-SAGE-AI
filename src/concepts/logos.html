(Concept) Logos

Six marks for Tinker Click.

Each one is drawn from something the site already says: the halo of strands, the line that becomes a halo, the plus of making and adding, apps as tiles, and the click itself. They keep the brand's blue and orange.

Shown large on light and dark, as an app icon, at favicon sizes, and next to the wordmark. Copy SVG puts the mark on the clipboard.

The thinking orb, held still.

Made from the halo itself: the same strands, from the same code that draws the orb on the page, frozen at one moment. From the whole orb down to a few strokes, each strand a comet, thin and faint at its tail, full at its head.

H1The whole orb

All 150 strands, exactly as the hero draws them, in the ink colour. The real thing, for large uses: the site, a poster, a launch screen.

  • The real orb
  • Large sizes only
  • Pair with a simpler one

H2Twelve strands

Only the twelve brightest strands, a little heavier, in blue. Still unmistakably the orb, light enough to sit by the wordmark.

  • Balanced
  • Works at icon size
  • Airy

H3Five brush strokes

The five brightest strands as bold strokes that swell from a fine tail to a full head, like a circle drawn quickly with a brush.

  • Bold
  • Strong when tiny
  • Hand-made

H4In two colours

Nine strands in the brand blue with two in orange running among them, like the idea woven into the thinking.

  • Brand colours
  • Lively
  • Medium weight

H5One strand, going round

A single strand spiralling in a little over once round, from a fine orange tail outside to a full blue head inside, like the orb drawn in one stroke without lifting the pen.

  • Simplest
  • Clearest at 16px
  • Movement

To tinker: to make it better.

The orbit again, round five ways of showing improvement: each one something getting a little better, step by step, the way tinkering does.

ISteps up

Three bars, each taller than the last, the newest in orange: every version a step better than the one before.

  • Instantly reads as better
  • Strong when tiny
  • A little corporate

IILevelling up

Three chevrons stacked upward, fading in from below to the orange one at the top, like a rank badge going up a level.

  • Playful
  • Game-like
  • Clear direction

IIIRough to smooth

A line that starts as a jagged scribble and smooths out as it goes, rising to an orange point: the first rough try, refined.

  • Tinkering, literally
  • Hand-made
  • Echoes the strands

IVIterations

A rough shape, a better one, then a perfect orange circle, each overlapping the last: three versions of the same idea, each one rounder.

  • Versions
  • Remix
  • Calm

VSpiralling up

A spiral that winds out from the centre and climbs away to an orange point: going round, a little further each time.

  • Iteration as a loop
  • Ties to the halo
  • Busier when small

The orbit, with a new heart.

06 kept, but without the two pluses, which can read as medical. The same ring passing behind and in front, round six other things the product is about.

AA spark

The four-point spark from the prompt box, where you describe your idea, with a small orange one beside it. The idea, caught in orbit.

  • The moment of the idea
  • Already in the UI
  • Reads as AI too

BA planet and its moon

A solid blue core with an orange moon riding the ring. Your app at the centre, things going round it. The simplest of the six.

  • Simplest
  • Clearest when tiny
  • Calm

CTwo app tiles

Keeps the overlap of the current mark, but as two rounded app tiles, blue and orange: one app, and the remix of it.

  • Closest to today
  • Apps, not crosses
  • Remix

DThe click

An orange pointer at the centre of the orbit: the Click in the name, with the halo going round it as if it had just set it off.

  • Says the name
  • Playful
  • Strong silhouette

EThe T

A heavy T, cut like the wordmark's letters, with an orange stem: a monogram that sits right next to TINKER.

  • Monogram
  • Pairs with Anton
  • Brand-first

FA stack of blocks

Three rounded blocks stacked a little off true, the top one orange: something being built, piece by piece.

  • Building
  • Tinkering
  • Friendly

The first six

01The halo

The orb that runs through the whole page, as a mark: a few open strokes going round, the way the strands do, with the orange plus at its centre where the idea is.

  • Ties to the site
  • Hand-drawn feel
  • Needs care when tiny

02Plus and click

The name in one picture: a bold plus for tinkering and adding, with a pointer clicking it. Reads instantly, even at 16px.

  • Literal
  • Strongest when small
  • Playful

03TC monogram

A heavy C drawn as one open stroke, like a strand of the halo, with an orange T set in its opening. A badge that works without the name.

  • Classic monogram
  • Bold
  • Pairs with Anton

04The app library

"A music library for apps, where you can add your own tracks": three app tiles and an empty fourth with a plus, waiting for the one you'll make.

  • Tells the product
  • Icon-grid friendly
  • Calm

05The thread

One continuous line that runs in, loops round into a halo and runs on, going from orange to blue: you describe it, it gets built, it goes out. The page's story in a stroke.

  • The page's story
  • Monoline
  • Distinctive

06The plus, in orbit

The mark you have now, the two overlapping pluses, with a single strand of the halo orbiting them, passing behind and then in front. An evolution rather than a new start.

  • Keeps what you have
  • Adds depth
  • Busier when small
