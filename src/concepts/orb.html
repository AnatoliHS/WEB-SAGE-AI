(Concepts) Orb

Two thinking orbs made of light, not dots.

Each one replaces the point-cloud sphere with a ring of thin strands that drift slowly along wavy paths, leaving an open centre. They're all white for now, on a dark ground so you can see them.

The Thinking slider changes the speed of every orb on the page, roughly what scrolling would do to the real one.
