(02) Meet Tinker

Make it. Remix it. Share it.

Think of it as a music library for apps, where you can add your own tracks. From a game of Go to a working copy of Winamp, the apps people make in Tinker run right inside it.

{% endmacro %} {% macro pillars() %}
01

Discover

Hundreds of public apps are ready to use as starting points. Type /discover or open the Gallery to explore a world of ideas.

02

Remix

Open someone's app to get your own copy, then change it however you like, even translate it into your language.

03

Share when you're ready

Your apps stay private until you decide otherwise. When you're proud of something, share it with the world in one step.

{% endmacro %}

A

The Shelf

Two rows of app cards drift past in opposite directions, like records on a shelf. Every card shows the sentence that made it, so the takeaway is "every app started as one line". Hovering pauses the shelf and lifts a card.

  • Ambient motion
  • Hover to pause
  • Shows breadth

B

Now Playing

This takes the "music library for apps" line literally. A player cycles through public apps like tracks, with a cover, the maker, the original ask and a progress bar. Visitors can skip, pause or pick from the queue, and the Remix button points straight at pillar 02.

  • Owns the metaphor
  • Interactive
  • One app at a time

    Remix Chain

    One app's journey, played out in three cards that line up with the pillars below. Someone's public game is copied into your library, a typed request turns it pink and German, and a switch flips it public while the remix count climbs. It replays whenever it scrolls into view.

    • Maps 1:1 to pillars

    (Compare)

    Side by side.

    ConceptWhat it saysMotionFits the brandEffortPhonesWatch out for
    AThe Shelf "There's a lot here, and it all started as a sentence." Constant, ambient ●●●●● Low Great, swipe-friendly Can feel generic, since many sites use marquees
    BNow Playing "It's a library for apps, so press play." Timed, every 5s ●●●●● Medium Good, stacks vertically Shows one app at a time, so less sense of scale
    CRemix Chain "Here's exactly how Discover, Remix and Share work." Scripted story, loops ●●●●● Medium Good, cards stack Repeats the pillar text, so trim the pillar copy
    D/discover "This is the real app, and it's this easy to find things." Typing, then filtering ●●●●● Medium OK, 2-column grid Needs real cover images to feel authentic

    Recommendation: C, Remix Chain. It's the only concept that explains the three pillars rather than decorating them. Its cards sit directly above 01, 02 and 03, and it shows remixing and translation, which are Tinker's most distinctive features. A works well as a lighter fallback, or as a separate full-width band elsewhere on the page.
