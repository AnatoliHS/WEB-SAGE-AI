(Concept) Progress lines

How far through the cards.

Five takes on the line under the features rail, each under a miniature of the rail, with the line on the page now at the top for comparison. The slider stands in for scrolling through the cards and drives every one at once.

Each takes the colour of the card the rail has come to, as now. It plays on a loop; drag the slider to scrub.

Card 1 of 7

00As it is now

About thirty strands, each with its own wave, weaving over and under one another and tapering at both ends.

  • Live today

01A single thread

One clear strand with a soft wave and a glowing head, three faint ones keeping it company. The quietest; reads at a glance.

  • Calm
  • Clearest progress

02A braid

Three strands twisting round one another in an even rope, each brighter as it comes to the front. Made, not scribbled.

  • Crafted
  • Regular rhythm

03A small halo per card

A faint track the whole length, one strand along it, and a tiny halo winding up at each card's end as the line passes it, in that card's colour.

  • Shows which card
  • Echoes the halo

04A pulse at the front

A faint track and a calm, straight line behind; all the movement is a burst of waves right at the head, as if it were pulling the line along.

  • Energy where it matters
  • Calm behind

05A trail of particles

No lines at all: particles streaming along behind the head, thick and bright at the front and thinning out behind, as the orb's explosion left them.

  • Ties to the explosions
  • Soft edges
