(Concept) Orb steps

The halo builds the app.

A copy of the Meet Tinker demo, made scroll-driven: the slider stands in for scrolling, and each step gets a quarter of it. The particles from the explosion fly back together into the hero halo above the prompt, then the halo does the work of each step, taking the step's colour.

It plays on a loop; drag the slider to scrub.

  • Becomes the interface
  • Colour per step
  • Scroll-driven

01 Describe
  1. 01 Describe

    The particles gather into the halo above the prompt, in the prompt's blue, while the idea types out.

  2. 02 Build

    The strands shoot out and run round the app window, header and tiles as they appear, and keep tracing them while it builds.

  3. 03 Remix

    The strands move in to the blocks of text being translated and run round them, as they ran round the app while it built.

  4. 04 Share

    The strands split in two and fly in straight lines into the other two cards, winding up there as small halos.
