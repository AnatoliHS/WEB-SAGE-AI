(Concept) FAQ flow

From the progress line to the footer halo.

Each panel is a miniature of the bottom of the page: the features' progress line, finished, then the FAQ, then the footer. The slider stands in for scrolling from where the rail unpins down to the bottom of the page, and drives every panel at once so they can be compared at the same moment.

It plays on a loop; drag the slider to scrub.

Scroll 0%

00As it is now

The line scrolls away with the rail, and as the footer comes up the particles fly in from all over the screen and implode into the halo. Here for comparison.

  • Live today
  • No link between the two

01The line peels off and flows down

The finished line unwinds from its tip: its strands come loose as particles and stream down the gap between the title and the questions, then curve into the halo's spot.

  • One continuous thread
  • Stays out of the text
  • Effort: medium

02Threads along the dividers

The strands leave the line one after another and run along each divider in turn, zigzagging down the list, then drop into the footer and wind up as the halo.

  • Uses lines already there
  • Crosses behind the questions
  • Effort: medium

03A spiral arrival

Where the particles come from stays as now, but they arrive on a tightening spiral, and the halo is still spinning as it forms, slowing to its usual drift.

  • A deliberate ending
  • Combines with 01 or 02
  • Effort: low

04A reaction to an open question

Opening a question pulls a few particles into a tiny ring round its icon; when it closes they drift on down and join the halo. Here the second question opens and closes on its own.

  • Feedback, not guidance
  • Combines with any
  • Effort: low

0501 + 03 Recommended

The line peels off and streams down the gap, and the stream spirals into the footer, the halo spinning up as it lands. The particles that tracked the cards become the halo.

  • One continuous thread
  • A deliberate ending
  • Effort: medium
