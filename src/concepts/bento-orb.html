(Concepts) Bento orb

Three ways to carry the orb through the features.

Each replaces the magnifier with the orb from the sections above, and cuts the bento down to the four step colours. A card only shows its colour while the orb is lighting it, so colour keeps meaning the same thing down the page.

They play on their own here. On the real page, they'd run while the bento is on screen; on phones, the card in the middle of the screen would be the lit one.
