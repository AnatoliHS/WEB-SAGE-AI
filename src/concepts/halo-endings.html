(Concept) Halo endings

The halo, one last time.

Five ways for the halo to arrive in the footer at the end of the page, each on a miniature of the footer. They all finish on the halo as it is now, drifting on its own, so any of them can stand in for the still one.

Each plays once the footer comes into view, then holds; here they loop together. Drag the slider to scrub.

0%

01Sketched in

The strands draw themselves one after another, each a pen stroke round the circle, until the whole halo is there. Like someone drawing it by hand.

  • Hand-made
  • Calm

02Spun out from a point

It starts as a bright point and unfurls, spinning fast, slowing as it opens out to its full size and settling into its drift.

  • Energetic
  • A finale

03A line opening into a circle

It starts as a flat line, like the progress line, and opens upwards and downwards into the round halo, like an eye opening.

  • Echoes the progress line
  • Simple

04Closing in from a wide orbit

Faint strands circling wide round the spot, as the orb's did on the stage, draw in and brighten until they're the halo.

  • Echoes the stage
  • Gentle

05One last lap

The halo fades up, then a single bright comet runs once round it and is gone, and the halo takes a breath. A full stop for the page.

  • A clear ending
  • Subtle
