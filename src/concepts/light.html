(Concepts) Bento lamp

A light over the Features grid.

Each version uses the real Features cards. A is the soft glow from before. B to E turn the light into a solid circle that follows your cursor: everything inside it is shown in another colour, with a clean edge that cuts straight across the cards. On phones, and when the cursor leaves, the circle drifts on its own.

  • A Glow
  • B Flood
  • C Ink Lens
  • D One Colour
  • E Magnifier
  • Compare
A

Glow

The previous version, for reference. A soft light in each card's colour, with rims that light up nearest the cursor.

  • Soft
  • Before
{{ lamp("glow") }}
B

Flood

Inside the circle, every card is flooded with its own colour and the text turns dark, the same look the site already uses on hover. Now it's a lens: one circle can cut across three cards at once, showing three colours meeting at the gaps.

  • Uses the existing hover look
  • Multi-colour
  • Hard edge
{{ lamp("flood") }}
C

Ink Lens

The circle inverts the page: inside it everything goes dark (light in dark mode), and only the titles and the language line keep their colours, so they glow. It's the most restrained version, and it feels like looking at the grid through a dark glass.

  • Monochrome
  • Colours pop
  • Works in both themes
{{ lamp("ink") }}
D

One Colour

The closest to the hellohello light. Everything inside the circle, gaps included, turns brand orange, with the cards a shade deeper and all text in ink. It reads as one strong spotlight rather than seven colours.

  • Brand orange
  • Spotlight
  • Boldest
{{ lamp("solo") }}
E

Magnifier

B's colour flood, plus a slight zoom inside the circle and a thin ink ring around it, so it behaves like a magnifying glass you slide over the cards. It's the most playful version, a nice nod to tinkering.

  • Zoom
  • Ring
  • Playful
{{ lamp("lens") }}

Side by side.

ConceptInside the circleBoldnessFits the siteEffortWatch out for
AGlowSoft light, no circle●●●●●●●●●●LowA common pattern, so it doesn't feel like ours
BFloodEach card in its own colour●●●●●●●●●●LowReplaces the current per-card hover rather than adding to it
CInk LensInverted, colours kept●●●●●●●●●●LowBody text inside the lens is dimmer, so keep the circle moving rather than parked
DOne ColourEverything brand orange●●●●●●●●●●LowHides the seven card colours, which are the grid's personality
EMagnifierColour flood, zoomed●●●●●●●●●●LowThe zoom makes text inside move, so it's harder to read while moving

Recommendation: B, Flood. It turns the colour fill the cards already have into a lens, so it feels like it belongs, and the moments where one circle cuts across three colours are the striking part. E is the same idea with more play, if the page can take it. On the live site, the lens would replace the current per-card hover, and on phones it could sit still until the grid scrolls into view.
