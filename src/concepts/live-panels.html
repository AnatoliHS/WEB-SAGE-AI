Recommendation: B+C. The 3D build and the fanned-out share are the moments that explain the product, and swapping Layers' tile flip for Editorial's word roll makes Remix calmer and makes the translation itself the point. D stays the softer alternative if the colourful tiles feel too loud next to the orb.
